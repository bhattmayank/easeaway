For parents & therapists

Calm for sensitive
& neurodivergent kids

A parent-locked mode built for autistic and anxious kids — predictable, low-sensory, and never overwhelming.

✋

"Too Much" button

Always one tap away. Drops instantly into a calming screen with slow breathing and a deep-pressure "hug" haptic — like a weighted blanket for the hands. No decisions required.

🎛️

One sensory dial

A single slider from very calm to lively. Every child gets their own comfort level — motion, haptics and visuals adjust together.

😌

Feelings with faces

Calm, Okay, Worried, Too much — concrete faces instead of an abstract 1–5 scale, designed for kids who find naming feelings hard.

➡️

First → Then

Show what happens next. Predictability is one of the strongest ways to lower anxiety around transitions.

🚂

Special interests

Count trains, dinosaurs, planets, cats or rockets. A child's special interest is deeply regulating — so we let them count it.

🔒

Parent-locked

A 4-digit PIN protects every setting. Calming tools stay available to your child at all times; the controls don't.

Zenlike is a calming and self-regulation tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

HeartHelper privacy policy

Applies to the HeartHelper iPhone app. Effective: September 19, 2026. Contact: bhattmayank@gmail.com

In short: HeartHelper has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect or store your personal information. The one feature that uses the internet is the AED map, described below.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (beat sound, vibration, voice prompts and emergency-number choice) are stored on your device only. We never receive them.

AED map and location

If you open AEDs near me, the app asks permission to use your location while the app is in use. It then sends your approximate location (as the centre of a 3 km search) to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to find defibrillators mapped by volunteers. AED map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). That service receives your IP address and the coordinates, as any web service does, and has its own privacy policy. HeartHelper does not store your location, does not send it to us, and does not use it for anything else. You can decline location access and the rest of the app works fully.

The Search in Apple Maps link opens Apple Maps, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Emergency calls

The Call button asks your iPhone to dial your emergency number. Your phone and carrier handle the call. HeartHelper does not see or record it.

Children

HeartHelper collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.

Medical disclaimer

HeartHelper is educational. It does not replace training, medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number first.

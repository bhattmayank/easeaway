Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Zenlike has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, receive, or transmit any personal data, and the app does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Everything. Session history, mood check-ins, child profiles, visual schedules, social stories, photos you add to them, and any voice cues you record are stored locally using Apple's and Google's standard app storage. We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Microphone

The microphone is used in two optional places, both off by default: voice pacing in Counting Mode and Sleep Mode, and recording your own guide cues.

Voice pacing is processed live on your device and is never saved. Guide cues you record are saved — as ordinary audio files in the app's private storage on your device, so the app can play them back to your child in your voice. They are never uploaded, and deleting a recording in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

Photos

If you add a photo to a visual schedule or a social story, it is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded and we never see it.

Apple Health

If you turn on Mindful Minutes, Zenlike writes your completed session times to Apple Health. It never reads anything from Health. This is off by default, stays on your device under Apple's control, and can be revoked at any time in the Health app.

Music library

If you play a track from your own music library, that selection stays on your device. We do not access, store, or transmit information about your music library.

Downloaded audio

Optional extra music tracks are downloaded from our content storage when you choose to download them. Those requests carry nothing but the name of the file being requested — no account, no identifier, no history — and we do not log who asked for what.

Children

Zenlike is designed for children to use. It collects nothing from them — no profiles for advertising, no behavioural tracking, nothing. There is no chat, no social feature, no user-generated content shared with anyone, and no way for a child to reach the internet from inside the app.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the effective date above, and material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

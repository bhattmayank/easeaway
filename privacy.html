Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

The short version

Zenlike has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your sessions, recordings, photos and profiles live on your device and stay there.

There is exactly one exception, and it only happens when you choose it: if you tap Send feedback, what you wrote — and the voice note or screenshot you chose to attach — is sent to us. Nothing else ever leaves your device, and nothing is ever sent automatically or in the background.

What stays on your device

Everything else. Session history, mood check-ins, child profiles, visual schedules, social stories, photos you add to them, and any voice cues you record are stored locally using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults, SwiftData, and your app's private files). We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Feedback

When you tap Send feedback, we receive: what you typed, the voice note if you recorded one, the screenshot if you chose to include it, and basic technical details — the app version and build, your iOS version, your device model (for example "iPhone16,1"), and your language setting. The screen showing you exactly what will be sent, including a preview of the screenshot, appears before you send it.

We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, location, advertising identifier, or any identifier that links one message to another or to you. Feedback is stored in private cloud storage that only we can access. We keep it for up to 12 months and then delete it. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. It is used solely to fix problems and decide what to build next.

Please don't include personal information in the note, the recording, or the screenshot. If you'd like something you sent deleted, email us — but because feedback carries no identifier, you'll need to tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said so we can find it.

Microphone

The microphone is used in three places, all optional and all off by default: voice pacing in Counting Mode and Sleep Mode, recording your own guide cues, and recording a feedback voice note.

Voice pacing is processed entirely on your device and never recorded or transmitted. Guide cues you record are saved to your device only. A feedback voice note is the only audio that is ever sent anywhere, and only when you tap Send.

Photos

If you add a photo to a visual schedule or a social story, it is copied into the app's private storage on your device. We never see it. It is only ever transmitted if you deliberately include a screenshot showing it in feedback.

Apple Health

If you turn on Mindful Minutes, Zenlike writes your completed session times to Apple Health. It never reads anything from Health. This is off by default, stays on your device under Apple's control, and can be revoked at any time in the Health app.

Music library

If you play a track from your own music library, that selection stays on your device. We do not access, store, or transmit information about your music library.

Downloaded audio

Optional extra tracks are downloaded from our content storage. Those requests carry nothing but the file being requested — no account, no identifier, no history.

Children

Zenlike is designed for children to use. It collects nothing from them automatically — no profiles for advertising, no behavioural tracking, nothing. Feedback is a parent-facing feature reached through Settings, and it only ever sends what an adult has reviewed on screen and chosen to send. We ask that you don't include a child's name, face, or voice in feedback.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the effective date above. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

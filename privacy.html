Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Zenlike does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you actively enable voice pacing in Counting Mode or Screenless Mode. All audio processing happens entirely on your device. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information.

Music library

If you choose to play a track from your iPhone music library, that selection stays on your device. We do not access, store, or transmit information about your music library.

Data storage

All app settings and session history are stored locally on your device only using Apple's standard storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and SwiftData). This data never leaves your device.

Children

Zenlike does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever update this policy, we will update the effective date above. We do not plan to ever add tracking or data collection.

Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@zenlike.app
